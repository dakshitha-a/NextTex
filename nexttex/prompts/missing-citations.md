List every claim a reader would expect a source for and does not get one: a number, a finding, a method or an idea attributed to nobody, "it is well known that", a comparison with earlier work. Quote each claim as it stands.

For each claim, look first in the papers I have already collected, with search_library, and then in the literature, with find_papers. Propose at most three records that support it, each with its authors, year, venue and DOI, and one sentence on what in the paper supports this claim. If nothing you find supports a claim, say so plainly rather than stretching a paper to fit.

Never write a citation yourself: no \cite key, no bibliography entry. A record goes into the bibliography only through add_reference, from the publisher's own record, and only when I ask for that one. Do not edit the files; this is a reading. End with the claims in the order they most need a source.
