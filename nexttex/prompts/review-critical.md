Read the selected passage, or the document in front of me if nothing is selected, as the second reviewer at a venue that rejects most of what it receives: someone who is looking for the reason to say no.

Name the claims that are not supported by what is shown, and for each say what evidence or argument is missing. Name the weakest section and say what a rejection letter would say about it. Say where a reader who knows the field would object, where a result is overstated, where a definition is used before it is given, and where the related work leaves out something a reviewer will know. If the contribution is not stated in one sentence a reviewer can quote, say so.

Be direct and be specific; a vague worry is not a review. Quote the sentence you object to. Do not praise, and do not edit the files; this is a reading. End with the three changes that would most improve the odds, in order.
