% A paper for an Elsevier journal, on Elsevier's own class, elsarticle.
%
% `preprint` is the single-column format for submission.  For a sense of the
% printed page, `final,3p` or `final,5p,twocolumn` give one and two columns.
% The bibliography style is Elsevier's numbered one; the journal's guide for
% authors says if it wants names and years instead.

\documentclass[preprint,12pt]{elsarticle}

\usepackage{amsmath}
\usepackage{graphicx}

\journal{The Journal}

\begin{document}

\begin{frontmatter}

\title{A Working Title}

\author[inst]{Your Name}
\ead{you@example.org}
\affiliation[inst]{organization={Your Institution},
  city={City},
  country={Country}}

\begin{abstract}
One paragraph saying what the work is, what you did, and what you found.
Write it last.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keyword}
first keyword \sep second keyword
\end{keyword}

\end{frontmatter}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Start here.  A citation looks like this~\cite{knuth1984}, and a reference to
another section looks like Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

A numbered equation:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:example}
  E = mc^2.
\end{equation}

\section{Conclusion}

What the results mean, and what they do not.

\bibliographystyle{elsarticle-num}
\bibliography{references}

\end{document}
