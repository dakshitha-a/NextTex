# The listing

Paste the posting here, or the parts of it you will write to: the title, the team, what they ask for, and the closing date. The resume and the cover letter beside this file are written against it, and a reader of this folder a year from now will want to know what the two were for.

Company, job title, and where you saw it.

Closing date, and the date you sent it.

## What they ask for

The requirements, in the words the listing uses, so the resume can use the same words.

## Notes

Who you spoke to, what they said, and what to say in the interview.
