% A reply to the reviewers, one point at a time.
%
% Each \point is what a reviewer said, \quoted the words of the paper it
% is about, \source where they are (a Ctrl-click in NextTex opens them),
% and the reply environment is your answer. The Comments drawer's
% "Write the reply letter" adds one point for every open comment thread.
% Send it with the marked-up PDF History gives: a version's menu, Changes
% as PDF.
\documentclass[11pt]{article}
\usepackage[T1]{fontenc}
\usepackage{lmodern}
\usepackage[margin=1in]{geometry}
\usepackage{parskip}

\newcounter{point}
\newcommand{\point}[1]{%
  \medskip\refstepcounter{point}\noindent\textbf{Point~\thepoint.}\ #1\par}
\newcommand{\quoted}[1]{\begin{quote}\itshape #1\end{quote}}
\newcommand{\source}[1]{\noindent{\small\ttfamily\detokenize{#1}}\par}
\newenvironment{reply}{\noindent\textbf{Reply.}\ }{\par}

\title{Reply to the reviewers}
\author{Your Name}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

We thank the reviewers for their careful reading. Each point is answered
below; the changes are marked in the accompanying PDF.

\section*{Reviewer 1}

\point{The first point the reviewer made.}
\begin{reply}
What you changed, and where.
\end{reply}

\end{document}
